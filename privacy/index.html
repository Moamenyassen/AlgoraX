Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you contact us through this website we receive what you type into the form: your name, email, phone number if you add it, company, and your message. We do not use advertising trackers.

Why we use it

Only to reply to you and to follow up on what you asked about. We do not sell or share your details for marketing.

Form messages are delivered to our inbox by EmailJS. WhatsApp and email conversations are handled by those services under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep messages as long as we are in contact with you, and delete them on request.

Your choices
